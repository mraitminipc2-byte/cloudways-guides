What is Cloudways?

Cloudways is managed cloud hosting: it puts a beginner-friendly dashboard on top of serious infrastructure — DigitalOcean, Vultr, AWS, and Google Cloud. You get the speed of a VPS with the Lightning Stack (NGINX, Varnish, Redis), free SSL, automated backups, 1-click vertical scaling, and 24/7 support — without ever touching a terminal. Plans start around $11–14/month on the DigitalOcean tier, with a 3-day free trial that needs no credit card.

Good to know before signing up: Cloudways doesn't register domains, email hosting is a paid add-on, and pricing differs by cloud provider — check the exact plan for the provider you pick.
