Cloudways vs Shared Hosting (and Raw VPS): Why the Switch Pays Off

Based on the video "Cloudways vs Shared Hosting: Why I Switched" by MR AIT · watch on YouTube

MR AIT quit raw, unmanaged VPS servers and moved his projects to Cloudways. This lesson is the honest reasoning: where shared hosting breaks, where unmanaged VPS bites back, and what managed hosting actually buys you.

Where shared hosting falls short

Shared hosting means your site lives on a server with dozens of strangers, on a stack you can't tune. When traffic arrives, resources are contested — the classic crash-during-a-spike story — and "upgrading" often means a confusing tier ladder rather than more real power.

The hidden risks of raw DigitalOcean & Vultr

Raw VPS is the opposite extreme: full power, full responsibility. The video calls out the traps:

  • You are the sysadmin — NGINX, PHP, database, certificates, firewall: all manual, all breakable.
  • Surprise bills — misconfigured sizes, forgotten resources, and bandwidth overages on unmanaged accounts turn into unexpected charges.
  • Constant maintenance — updates and security patches land on your plate, forever.

What managed hosting changes

  • Deploy an optimized WordPress/PHP server in minutes — the Lightning Stack is provisioned for you.
  • 1-click vertical scaling — bump RAM/CPU instantly when a traffic spike hits; downgrade when it passes. Pay-as-you-go means you only pay for what you use.
  • Free SSL & automated backups — security fundamentals are built in, not add-ons.
  • 24/7 support — expert help when the server misbehaves instead of forum archaeology.
The fair comparison: raw VPS from DigitalOcean or Vultr is cheaper per raw spec. Managed hosting costs a premium for the dashboard, the stack, the backups, and the support. The video's verdict: for anyone without a sysadmin on call, the premium is the cheaper option once your time and downtime are counted.
