Cloudways Pricing Explained: What You Actually Pay in 2026

A reference guide based on MR AIT’s Cloudways video course and current public reviews

Cloudways pricing confuses newcomers because there isn’t one price — there’s a price per cloud provider and server size. Here’s the map, using the numbers public reviews report as of late 2026.

The price list, decoded

  • DigitalOcean (standard) 1 GB — roughly $11/month (reviews cite $10–14 depending on source and billing period). This is the entry point most beginners start on, and the tier the course’s walkthroughs use.
  • Premium DigitalOcean — about 27% more than standard for faster CPU/NVMe on the same RAM.
  • Vultr / Linode tiers — comparable to DigitalOcean with slight positioning differences.
  • AWS and Google Cloud — the same RAM costs noticeably more; you’re paying for enterprise-grade infrastructure and network reach.

You pay by the hour, not by the year

Cloudways bills pay-as-you-go: the monthly price is pro-rated hourly, and 1-click vertical scaling changes the rate immediately in both directions. Spin a server up for a weekend test and you pay for a weekend — that’s also why the free trial and coupon credits stretch so far.

What the managed premium buys

A raw DigitalOcean droplet with identical specs costs roughly half the Cloudways price. The difference is the managed layer: the Lightning Stack (NGINX, Varnish, Redis) installed and tuned, automated backups, free SSL, security patching, the dashboard, and 24/7 support. Whether that’s worth double is the managed-vs-unmanaged question — the comparison guide breaks it down.

The extras people miss

  • Domains aren’t included — register yours anywhere and point it at Cloudways.
  • Email hosting is an add-on — transactional or mailbox email runs through a paid add-on (Rackspace-style), not the plan price.
  • Taxes are excluded from listed prices in most regions.
  • Prices move with providers — Cloudways adjusts when the underlying clouds do, so confirm the current number for your chosen provider at checkout.
