Cloudways vs Raw Vultr & DigitalOcean: Managed vs Unmanaged VPS

A reference guide based on MR AIT’s Cloudways video course and current public reviews

Here’s the comparison most reviews dodge: Cloudways runs on top of DigitalOcean and Vultr — same datacenters, same hardware. The difference is who does the work. This guide puts the two paths side by side.

The cost, honestly stated

A raw DigitalOcean droplet with the same specs costs roughly half the Cloudways plan — the difference is the managed fee. Whether that fee is “wasted” depends entirely on what your hour is worth and what happens when the server breaks at 2 a.m.

Side by side

  • Setup — Cloudways: pick app, provider, size, launch; the optimized stack is provisioned for you (the 5-minute launch). Raw VPS: install the stack yourself, harden it, tune it — hours of specialist work before WordPress exists.
  • Maintenance — Cloudways: patches, updates, and monitoring are handled. Raw VPS: that’s your permanent part-time job.
  • Scaling — Cloudways: 1-click vertical scaling, up or down, billed hourly. Raw VPS: resize the droplet yourself, still manage everything on it.
  • Security & backups — Cloudways: free SSL and automated backups included. Raw VPS: you choose, install, and verify every piece.
  • Surprise bills — the video’s recurring theme: misconfigured raw servers and forgotten resources generate unexpected charges. The managed layer keeps sizing and billing visible in one place.
  • Support — Cloudways: 24/7 team for the hosting layer. Raw VPS: community forums and your own debugging.

When raw VPS is the right call

If you enjoy server administration, need exotic configurations, or run dozens of servers where the managed fee genuinely adds up — go raw, and budget real time for it. The course’s author did exactly that for years before switching.

When Cloudways wins

When hosting is a means to an end: WordPress sites, client work, campaigns — anything where your time is better spent on the site than the server. That’s the throughline of the whole course, and the shared-hosting comparison and Hostinger comparison fill in the other angles.

Frequently asked questions

Is Cloudways just DigitalOcean with a markup?

Under the hood you get the same datacenters — the markup buys the managed layer: stack, backups, SSL, support, and the dashboard. You’re paying for the work you don’t do.

Can I start raw and move to Cloudways later?

Yes, and the reverse. The free migration method works from any host, raw or shared.

Can Cloudways use my existing DigitalOcean account?

The course’s workflow uses Cloudways’ own provider integration, where you pick DigitalOcean/Vultr/AWS/GCP at launch and Cloudways provisions and manages it.

Which is faster: Cloudways or raw DigitalOcean?

On identical specs, similar — the Lightning Stack (NGINX, Varnish, Redis) is the tuned configuration most admins would build themselves. Speed differences come from stack quality, not the dashboard.
