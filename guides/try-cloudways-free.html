Try Cloudways VPS for FREE: 2 Methods That Need No Commitment

Based on the video "Try Cloudways VPS for FREE – 2 Methods" by MR AIT · watch on YouTube

The smartest way to judge a hosting platform is to run your own test on it. This short lesson walks through the two ways MR AIT recommends for trying Cloudways VPS without commitment — no trial code gymnastics, no surprise charges.

Method 1: the 3-day free trial (no credit card)

Sign up directly on Cloudways and you get a 3-day free trial — no credit card and no phone number required. Just create the account and start testing. It's the fastest way to see the platform from the inside.

What you can test during the trial

  • Create VPS servers and delete them freely.
  • Install WordPress with one click.
  • Deploy other web applications.
  • Send test traffic and watch how the server holds up.
  • Experiment with bots and automation before you commit real workloads.

Method 2: $10–20 in free credits

The second method extends your testing window: use the sign-up link from the video (mrait.ca/go/vpsdealz) and redeem the coupon for $10–20 in free credits. This one needs a payment method on file — the video shows how to add it safely. The card is used for verification, not an immediate charge, and the credits cover your first server time.

Why bother testing Cloudways? It's managed hosting on top of serious infrastructure — DigitalOcean, Vultr, AWS, and Google Cloud — with a dashboard a beginner can actually use. Three days is enough to launch a server, install WordPress, and feel the difference from shared hosting.
