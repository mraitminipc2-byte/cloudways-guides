Cloudways Free Trial: 3 Days, No Credit Card (+ Bonus Credits)

A reference guide based on MR AIT’s Cloudways video course

You don’t have to take anyone’s word that Cloudways is good — the platform is built to be tested. Two no-commitment paths, both shown in Lesson 1.

Method 1: the 3-day trial, no card

Sign up directly and you get a 3-day free trial with no credit card and no phone number. Create the account, and the full dashboard is yours: launch servers, install WordPress, deploy apps, send test traffic, delete everything, start over.

Method 2: $10–20 in free credits

Use the course’s sign-up link (mrait.ca/go/vpsdealz) and redeem the coupon for $10–20 in credits — roughly the first month of the entry DigitalOcean server. This path needs a payment method on file, added safely as shown in the video: it’s a verification step, not a charge, and the credits cover server time first.

What to test during your trial

  • Launch a 1 GB DigitalOcean server and time the provisioning.
  • Install WordPress, log into wp-admin, poke around.
  • Check the Server Management area: monitoring, services, master credentials.
  • Issue a test SSL flow and look at the backup schedule.
  • Delete the server — and confirm billing behavior matches your expectations.

After the trial

Three days answers “do I like the platform?” The getting-started lesson then covers the real launch — provider choice, server size, and your first real WordPress install — and Lesson 4 migrates an existing site in when you’re ready.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the Cloudways free trial?

The standard trial is 3 days with no credit card required. The credits method effectively extends your testing window by covering the first month of a small server.

Do the credits expire?

Promotional credits carry their own terms that change over time — redeem them soon after signing up and confirm the current rules in your account’s billing page.

Can I use the trial and the credits together?

The two methods are alternatives shown in the video: the trial for a card-free test, the credits for a longer evaluation with a payment method on file. Pick the one that fits; the video shows both end to end.
