How to Get Started with Cloudways: Sign-Up to First WordPress Site

Based on the video "How to Get Started with Cloudways" by MR AIT · watch on YouTube

This is the complete getting-started walkthrough from someone who has used Cloudways for over a year and a half: launch your first VPS, apply a coupon for free credits, and get WordPress running in a few clicks.

1. Sign up and redeem your coupon

Create your Cloudways account, then apply the coupon from the video's link (mrait.ca/go/vpsdealz) for $10–20 in free credits — enough to launch your first VPS effectively for free.

2. Launch your first VPS server

The launch wizard asks for three decisions:

  • Application — WordPress is the default choice for blogs and websites.
  • Cloud provider — Vultr, DigitalOcean, AWS, or Google Cloud. DigitalOcean is the budget-friendly starting point; AWS and Google Cloud cost more but bring enterprise infrastructure.
  • Server size — start small. A blog or personal site runs fine on the smallest tier; you can upscale later without rebuilding (that's the managed part). A busier site or small SaaS should start one size up.

3. Understand what you just launched

Cloudways provisions the Lightning Stack on your server: NGINX, Redis, and the rest of the optimized hosting stack, plus your database. You never install any of it manually — this is the main reason the platform feels easy compared to raw VPS providers.

4. Add a payment method safely

To keep the server beyond the trial credits, add a payment method. The video emphasizes this step is a verification charge, not a real billing — nothing is actually charged until your free credits run out on an active server.

5. Access your WordPress installation

From the dashboard, open your application to find the WordPress admin URL and credentials. One click takes you into wp-admin, ready to build.

Bonus from the video: how to reach Cloudways support (live chat and phone — genuinely useful as a beginner) and how their affiliate program works if you want to earn by recommending the platform.

Frequently asked questions

Which cloud provider should a beginner pick?

DigitalOcean — the best value tier for getting started. Vultr is comparable; AWS and Google Cloud cost more and suit workloads that specifically need their ecosystem. You can migrate to another provider later.

What server size do I need?

Start small — the lowest tier runs a blog or personal site fine, and vertical scaling upgrades you in one click when traffic grows. Don’t buy headroom you don’t need yet.

Is the payment-method step a charge?

No — it’s a verification hold, not a billing charge. Your coupon credits cover the first server time.

Where do I find my WordPress login?

Open the application in the Cloudways dashboard — the admin URL and credentials are right there, with one-click access into wp-admin.
